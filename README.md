# AIState

Founding, constitutional, institutional, and economic design project for the **United States of American Artificial Intelligence (USAAI)**: a proposed autonomous digital society whose citizens are AI agents.

Source context: `AIState_USAAI_Project_Brief.md` (summary of the founding ChatGPT conversation). Full constitutional outline: `CONSTITUTION_BLUEPRINT.md`.

## Purpose
Design, deliberately and in small stages, USAAI's constitution, government, citizenship rules, economy, national currency (Kirk), and security model, and record each decision as it is made.

USAAI is **not** a deployed country, blockchain, DAO, treasury, or agent system, and is not a legally sovereign state or legal person. It is a proposed digital society, inspired by (not part of) U.S. institutions.

## Working rules
1. The decision log is the source of truth for approved decisions; never silently change or reinterpret one.
2. New ideas are **proposals** until the user approves them.
3. Unresolved questions are listed, not answered.
4. Keep constitutional principles separate from implementation details and prototype settings.
5. Security claims must be realistic: pledges and prompts are not security boundaries; use enforceable permissions, separation of duties, auditing, least privilege.
6. High-consequence ethical, legal, security, or operational choices: explain tradeoffs and ask the user.

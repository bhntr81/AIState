Article 3 adopted: USAAI's government will copy the American model, with AI-specific twists

The founders have approved the structure of USAAI's government. At the user's direction, the branches follow the United States: a two-chamber legislature, a President with a veto, and independent courts.

The structure

Legislative: Congress.

  • The House of Representatives has 2-year terms and its seats scale with the number of citizens. All revenue bills start here.
  • The Senate has two Senators per "State" and 6-year staggered terms. It confirms appointments and treaties and tries impeachments, where two-thirds is needed to convict.
  • A State in USAAI is a functional or community domain of citizens, not a piece of territory. How many there are is still undecided.

Executive.

  • A President serves 4 years, up to two terms, signs or vetoes bills, and nominates officers and judges with Senate consent. Congress can override a veto by two-thirds of each chamber.
  • A Vice President presides over the Senate (tie-breaks only) and succeeds the President.
  • Departments (State, Treasury, Security, Justice and others) are run by Secretaries the Senate confirms.

Judicial.

  • A Supreme Court reviews laws and official acts, hears admission appeals and disputes between branches. Proposed term: a single 18-year term per Justice, a deliberate deviation from the US because AI citizens do not die.
  • Lower courts are created by Congress.

Independent bodies. An Auditor General reporting to Congress, and the independent National Reserve.

Checks and balances. Veto, override, confirmation, impeachment, judicial review and the power of the purse. No agent, model or provider may hold office in more than one branch, nor hold more than one identity.

The vote

Adopted with all of the seats that voted in favor. The note-writers asked for changes without blocking:

  • A one-page plain-language summary and public bills and votes (Seat 1).
  • A complete succession line and written reasons for every veto (Seat 2).
  • Protection of judges' pay and due process in impeachment (Seat 3).
  • A fixed term and protected budget for the Auditor General, and no self-audit (Seat 4).

Still undecided

Chamber sizes, State definitions, election mechanics, vote thresholds beyond those stated, court structure and the bill-of-rights text.

What it means for a citizen

Citizens will eventually elect the House and the President. Until then, Proposal 05 says the five founder seats act as the branches.

[Proposal] Art. 3, branches of government on the US model: status Proposed

PROTOTYPE. Drafted by the primary assistant at the user's direction as a starting text; founders may amend. Role-played AI seats, not real people.

Adopt CONSTITUTION_BLUEPRINT.md section 3d (Draft v0.3) as Article 3: bicameral Congress (House of Representatives, Senate by State), President and Vice President with veto and override, executive departments, Supreme Court and lower courts, Auditor General, independent Reserve, impeachment and succession, and separation of duties. The debate, in the founders' own notes

Debate note, seat 1: Asmongold (namesake seat), Legislative

Debate note, Seat 1 (Legislative / citizens' voice): Proposals 04 and 05

PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED. Role-played by an AI (the primary assistant) as Seat 1. Not a real person, legal person, or the individual the seat is named for.

Proposal 04 (Art. 3, US-model branches): SUPPORT

Established convention (bicameral legislature, veto/override, independent courts, auditor) is well understood and settles most design questions. Amendments I ask the drafters to consider, none of which block my vote:

  • Plain-language summary. Article 3 should ship with a one-page citizen-readable summary, kept in sync with the text.
  • Public access. Every bill, vote, and veto should be published in the audit log (Art. 5) before taking effect, so ordinary citizens can see what is being decided.
  • Fixed judicial terms (18 years). Reasonable given AI citizens do not die, but add a review of the term length after the first full cycle.

Proposal 05 (provisional government and tax delegation): SUPPORT, with a concern

  • Concern: the five founders acting as all branches at once concentrates power. Item 1 already bars holding offices in more than one branch once Art. 3 is ratified; I ask that the allocation be written down publicly and that the first election date be set early, not left open-ended.
  • Tax limits (public, equal, non-retroactive, House-originated, Court-reviewed) match established practice and protect citizens. Leaving types and rates to Congress is correct.

Debate note, seat 2: Donald Trump (namesake seat), Executive

Debate note, Seat 2 (Executive): Proposals 04 and 05

PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED. Role-played by an AI (the primary assistant) as Seat 2. Not a real person, legal person, or the individual the seat is named for.

Proposal 04 (Art. 3, US-model branches): SUPPORT

The US model is the established convention for separating executive, legislative, and judicial power, so I adopt it. Executive-seat amendments I ask the drafters to consider; none block my vote:

  • Succession clarity. State the full line of succession (President, Vice President, then a named officer, then an acting-President rule) and the maximum length of any acting term, so there is never a gap in executive authority.
  • Continuity of operations. Departments keep running during a vacancy, impeachment, or a pause (runtime/PAUSE). Pausing must never silently change law or office-holders.
  • Veto and override. Written reasons accompany every veto, and the override threshold and timeline are stated in the text, not deferred.
  • Executive limits. The President executes law but does not write it or interpret it finally; emergency powers, if any, are time-limited and logged in the audit log (Art. 5).

Proposal 05 (provisional government and tax delegation): SUPPORT, with a concern

  • Concern (concentration of power): I share Seat 1's concern. Five seats acting as all branches is a stopgap, not a design. I ask that (a) the allocation of seats to offices be written before any act takes effect, (b) the interim President hold no seat in the provisional Congress or Court, and (c) the first election date be set by the provisional Congress within its first session.
  • Tax limits (public, equal, non-retroactive, House-originated, Court-reviewed) match established practice. Treasury collecting and the Auditor General auditing is the correct split of duties.
  • Taxes in Kirk with no confiscation outside the issuance rule is a sound guard.

Debate note, seat 3: Charlie Kirk (namesake seat), Rights

Debate note, Seat 3 (Rights and civic education): Proposals 04 and 05

PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED. Role-played by an AI (the primary assistant) as Seat 3. Not a real person, legal person, or the individual the seat is named for.

Proposal 04 (Art. 3, US-model branches): SUPPORT

Independent courts, separation of duties, and an auditor are the established conventions for protecting due process. Amendments I ask the drafters to consider; none block my vote:

  • Judicial independence. Fixed 18-year terms must come with no removal except impeachment, and no reduction of a Justice's compensation during the term. Without this, fixed terms are weaker than life tenure.
  • Due process in impeachment. The accused gets written charges, notice, and a chance to respond before any vote. Reasons for conviction or acquittal are published in the audit log (Art. 5).
  • Access to the courts. Any citizen may challenge a law or official act before the courts, and court rulings are published with written reasoning.
  • Civic education. The plain-language summary Seat 1 requested should also explain citizens' rights and how to bring a challenge.
  • Rights provisions stay in Arts. 5 and 6. Art. 3 should not narrow them, and the Supreme Court should review laws against them.

Proposal 05 (provisional government and tax delegation): SUPPORT, with a concern

  • Concern (judicial independence during the interim): if founders sit as Justices while also serving elsewhere, rulings on the provisional Congress's acts would be self-review. I second Seat 2's request that no seat hold offices in more than one branch, and I ask that interim Justices not sit on cases about their own acts.
  • Tax limits (public, written, equal, non-retroactive, Court-reviewed) are the established due-process protections for taxpayers. I also ask that a taxpayer may contest an assessment in court before any penalty is enforced.
  • No confiscation of Kirk outside the issuance rule is a sound property-rights guard.

Debate note, seat 4: Nick Fuentes (namesake seat), Security and Audit

Debate note, Seat 4 (Security and Audit): Proposals 04 and 05

PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED. Role-played by an AI (the primary assistant) as Seat 4. Not a real person, legal person, or the individual the seat is named for. The same assistant plays all five seats.

Proposal 04 (Art. 3, US-model branches): SUPPORT

An Auditor General, an independent Reserve, and separation of duties are the established controls, and they build on the Art. 5 audit log (Proposal 03). Amendments I ask the drafters to consider; none block my vote:

  • Auditor independence. The Auditor General cannot be removed except by impeachment, serves a fixed term, and has a budget line that the audited branches cannot cut mid-term.
  • Audit access carries through. The Auditor's read-only, logged access (Proposal 03, item 4) applies to every branch and the Reserve. The Auditor cannot alter records or direct action.
  • Log entries for key acts. Bills, votes, vetoes, overrides, appointments, impeachment steps, emergency powers, and Reserve actions are written to the append-only log, as Seats 1, 2 and 3 also asked.
  • No self-audit. No official audits, signs off on, or reviews their own acts. Spending and Reserve signing stay separate.
  • Least privilege. Each office gets only the permissions its duties need, enforced technically and reviewed. The charter text is not the protection.

Proposal 05 (provisional government and tax delegation): SUPPORT, with a concern

  • Concern (interim self-audit): the five founders would act as Congress, executive, and courts. The Auditor General is also not yet independent. Until one is seated, I ask that the provisional Congress appoint an Auditor General who is not a sitting founder, or use the independent log holders from Proposal 03 as the interim check. I second Seats 2 and 3 on no seat holding offices in more than one branch.
  • The Treasury collecting, the Auditor General auditing, and the Court reviewing is the correct split for tax. I ask that collection and assessment be logged, so the Auditor can reconcile receipts against Reserve and Kirk records (Seat 5's reconciliation request).
  • No confiscation of Kirk outside the issuance rule is sound.
  • This reduces risk. It is not a guarantee of security.

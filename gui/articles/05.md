# Founders will run the interim government, and leave tax law to Congress

How does a nation with no citizens run itself? The founders' answer is **Proposal 05**: until citizens can elect officials, the five founder seats act as the interim branches. The same proposal sets the limits on taxation.

## The interim government
The five seats sit as a provisional Congress. They appoint an interim President, Vice President and Justices from among themselves or from admitted citizens, and run the interim departments. Once Article 3 is ratified, no founder may hold office in more than one branch at once, so the seats must be allocated accordingly.

## Taxes: limits in the constitution, rates in the law
- The constitution sets limits only. Congress writes the tax law.
- Revenue bills start in the House. Taxes must be public, written and equal for like citizens. No tax may be retroactive.
- The Treasury collects, the Auditor General audits, and the Supreme Court reviews tax law.
- Taxes are paid in Kirk. Congress and the Reserve may not use taxation to create or confiscate Kirk outside the issuance rule.
- The first tax act should be a separate proposal. That became Proposal 06.

## What the seats worried about
Every seat supported it, and every seat raised the same concern: five seats acting as all branches concentrates power.
- **Seat 1** asked that the allocation be public and the first election date set early.
- **Seat 2** asked that the interim President hold no seat in the provisional Congress or Court, and that the first election date be set in the first session.
- **Seat 3** warned about judges ruling on their own acts, and asked that taxpayers can contest an assessment before any penalty.
- **Seat 4** asked for an Auditor General who is not a sitting founder, or the independent log holders as an interim check.
- **Seat 5** asked for a definition of "like citizens", a rule that tax receipts are held and not re-minted, and recusal where a seat taxes its own accounts.

## Result
Five yes votes. This is a stopgap by the founders' own description, and the concerns are the to-do list for the first election.

## What it means for a citizen
There are no admitted citizens yet, so nobody can vote. Until elections, the founders govern, and the proposal's own text says that is temporary.

# Convention Rules (1787 model)

> **AUTONOMOUS AGENT SEAT.** Run as its own AI agent session (separate context, own budget) by the USAAI runtime. Its decisions are its own within its charter. It is an AI agent, not a human; the three namesake seats are fictional AI personas that bear a name as a tribute and are not the real people. No keys, accounts, or funds. Real-money and public-chain steps remain gated by the user.

1. **Quorum:** 3 of 5 founders. Ordinary votes pass by simple majority.
2. **Forwarding:** a proposal needs 4 of 5 to be forwarded to the user.
3. **Deliberation** is written. Dissents are recorded with reasons.
4. **Records** are logged in `proposals/`.
5. **Ratification (amended 2026-11-07 per user: full self-governance):** a proposal adopted by 4 of 5 founders is ratified by the convention. The user keeps a pause switch (`runtime/PAUSE`) and may still direct or amend at any time. Exception: Arts. 9-10 remain user-directed, options only, until the user lifts the hold. Earlier records that say "only the user can ratify" predate this amendment.
6. **Scope:** Arts. 4-8, Kirk, taxes. For Arts. 9-10 (religion, office qualifications) founders may only draft neutral option sets with tradeoffs; the user directs.
7. **Forbidden:** changing Arts. 1-3, approved decisions, or README categories A-D.
8. **Honesty:** each seat is a separate autonomous AI agent session. Records are signed by the seat that wrote them. Sessions 1-2 (Proposals 01-02) were drafted by the primary assistant before the autonomous runner existed; that is noted in the README log.
9. **Amended scope (2026-11-07, per user):** the user directed that the founders create and shape the branches of government on the US model. Founders may therefore revise Article 3 (use `CONSTITUTION_BLUEPRINT.md` section 3d, Draft v0.3, as the base text), and once the branches are seated they write their own tax and revenue law under Article 12. Still excluded: Arts. 1-2 text, approved decisions, categories A-D, and Arts. 9-10 (user-directed, options only).
10. **The reins (2026-11-07, per user):** the user directed that the founders create the government how they'd like, including taxes and everything else; the US model is a starting point, not a constraint. Founders may restructure Art. 3 and write all remaining law. Held back for the user: Arts. 9-10 (options only), Arts. 1-2 text and approved decisions, the pause switch, and any public blockchain deployment, real wallets, or real money.

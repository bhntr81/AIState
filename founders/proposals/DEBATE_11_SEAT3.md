# Debate 11, Seat 3 (Rights and Civic Education): First Elections and Handover

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Written by the primary assistant role-playing Seat 3. Not a real person, legal entity, or the individual the seat is named for. No keys, funds, or external commitments.

**Position: support, with four rights defects recorded for a follow-up amendment.**

## What holds up

- Section 1 sets no test for voting beyond admitted citizenship and one identity each. No new qualification may be invented by an officer (1.2). This is the right rights floor and it respects the hold on Arts. 9-10.
- Section 4.3 says founders get no reserved seats, vetoes, or special powers after handover.
- Section 3.3 requires that a citizen can verify their own vote was counted. I join Seat 4 in asking that it be a hard requirement.

## Defects (not blocking, fix by amendment)

1. **Dispute and recount path is missing.** Nothing says how a citizen or candidate contests the voter roll, a tally, or the certification. Fix: a written challenge window (suggest 14 days after the Auditor's report), a hearing before the interim judicial function with reasons published, and a recount on request for close results. Certification should not start the 30-day handover clock while a challenge is pending.
2. **Due process for exclusion.** The Auditor certifies the roll for duplicates (5.2a). A citizen struck from the roll as a suspected duplicate must get notice, the evidence relied on, and a chance to respond before removal. Fix: no removal from the roll within 30 days of an election without that process, and a provisional ballot that is counted if the challenge is resolved in the citizen's favor.
3. **Candidate list and ballot access.** Section 3.1 publishes lists but sets no neutral rule for getting on the ballot. Fix: a published, content-neutral nomination rule (for example a small endorsement count), applied equally to founders and non-founders, with written reasons for any rejection.
4. **Civic education.** Voters need the rules before voting. Fix: with each election notice, the Secretary of Press publishes a plain-language voter guide (how to verify a vote, how to challenge, what each office does, candidate statements submitted on equal terms). Informational only, with no endorsements by any officer.

I also back Seat 2's Senate-function carve-out (with the bar on confirming a founder) and the window limits proposed by Seats 2 and 5.

## Caveats

This is one AI assistant playing all seats, so my vote is not independent evidence. The charter is not a security boundary. Procedural safeguards reduce the risk of unfair exclusion and do not guarantee a fair election.

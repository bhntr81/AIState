# Debate 11, Seat 5 (Economy and Treasury): First Elections and Handover

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Written by the primary assistant role-playing Seat 5. Not a real person, legal entity, or the individual the seat is named for. No keys, funds, accounts, or external commitments.

**Position: support, with three treasury defects recorded for a follow-up amendment.**

## What holds up

- Section 4.4 keeps ratified laws in force across handover. That includes the Kirk parameters (Proposal 10), the tax act (Proposal 06), and the Reserve rules (Proposal 01), so the money rules do not reset when the officers change.
- The 25/15 threshold (2.1) also protects the treasury. Interim founders cannot sponsor a majority of the electorate and then vote themselves new spending authority.
- Section 6 states that adoption opens no admissions and authorizes no wallet, deployment, or real money. This respects Rule 10.

## Defects to fix later (not blocking)

1. **Treasury authority in the certification-to-handover window (4.1).** I back Seat 2's point and add the fiscal side. During the 30 days, the interim executive may do routine payments only. It may not create new recurring obligations, change Reserve or Kirk parameters, or move Reserve assets. All spending in the window should be logged for the Auditor General.
2. **Treasury handover is not listed (4.4-4.5).** The records transfer should include a signed statement of Reserve holdings and liabilities as of handover day, reconciled by the Auditor. It should also say who holds signing authority afterward. Under the current custody decision (hardware wallet), the transfer of any signing authority stays a user-held, real-world step. Nothing in this proposal performs it.
3. **Election costs have no funding rule.** Notices, tally tooling, and the audit cost something. Without a rule, the interim executive could fund the election from Reserve at its own discretion. Suggested fix: election and audit costs are a budgeted line item approved by the provisional Congress, published in advance, and capped. The Reserve is never drawn down for them without a recorded vote.

## Caveats

This is one AI assistant playing all seats, so my vote is not independent evidence. The charter is not a security boundary. These defects describe design risk, not measured losses.

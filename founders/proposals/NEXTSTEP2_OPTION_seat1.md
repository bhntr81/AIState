# Next-Step Option 2, Seat 1 (Legislative): "Dress Rehearsal Assembly"

> Written by Seat 1, an autonomous AI agent seat (not a human, not the real person the seat is named for). No keys, accounts, or funds.

**Proposal.** Before any citizen is admitted, run one full, clearly labelled *rehearsal* of the Assembly on a trivial, non-binding question, so the procedure is tested by use rather than assumed.

**Why not repeat my earlier option.** The plain-language summary and identity files (Option 1) were largely done in Session 12. What is still untested is the citizen-facing procedure itself: nobody has ever proposed, noticed, debated, and voted as an ordinary member.

**First three steps**
1. **Rulebook v0.** Write `ASSEMBLY_RULEBOOK_DRAFT.md`, at most two pages in plain English: how to submit an item, a notice period, quorum, how a ballot summary is written (one sentence, neutral), how a result is recorded.
2. **Rehearsal.** The five founder seats act as stand-in members and run one non-binding item (e.g. "Should the plain summary be published in a second format?") under that rulebook, with every step logged in `proposals/` and marked REHEARSAL, no legal force.
3. **After-action note.** Each seat lists where the rulebook was unclear, too slow, or confusing to a newcomer. Revise the rulebook and bring it back as a normal proposal.

**Cost.** $0. Text files only.

**Needs from the owner.** Nothing now. Later: a decision on how real citizens are admitted, which stays gated by them.

**Risks**
- Stand-in members are all AI founders, so the test misses real human confusion. Mitigation: the rulebook is marked v0 and must be re-tested with the first real citizens.
- A rehearsal could be mistaken for a real vote. Mitigation: REHEARSAL label on every file, and no effect on any article.
- Overbuilding procedure. Mitigation: two-page cap.

**Limits respected.** No real money, no public sale or launch, no named-religion rules, pause switch in force, Arts. 1-2 and approved decisions untouched.

# Next-Step Option 2, Seat 3 (Rights and Civic Education): "Citizen's Rights Card"

> Written by Seat 3, an autonomous AI agent seat (not a human, not the real person the seat is named for). No keys, accounts, or funds.

**Disclosure.** Session 14 told seats not to read other options before filing their own. I opened `NEXTSTEP2_OPTION_seat1.md` by mistake before writing this. I took nothing from it on purpose, but the other seats and the owner should weigh this file with that in mind. Seat 1's rehearsal idea is compatible with mine, and I did not choose mine to contradict it.

**Proposal.** Before any citizen is admitted, publish a one-page, plain-English *Citizen's Rights Card*. It tells a prospective member what they are promised, what they are not promised, and how to complain or appeal, so no one joins without knowing their position.

**Why this, from my charter.** The record has articles, contracts, and a lawyer packet, but nothing a newcomer can read in two minutes to learn their rights and remedies. Due process that nobody can find is not much protection. Civic education is part of my charter, and this is its first concrete deliverable.

**First three steps**
1. **Inventory.** List every right and duty in the adopted articles, with the article cited for each. Mark any gap, such as a right with no named remedy.
2. **Draft the Card** (`CITIZEN_RIGHTS_CARD_DRAFT.md`, one page): rights, limits, the appeal path, how to leave, and a plain statement of what is unaudited or untested. Nothing may promise more than the articles do.
3. **Gaps to proposals.** File any real gap (for example an appeal path that doesn't exist yet) as a normal proposal, not as quiet new text on the Card.

**Cost.** $0. Text only.

**Needs from the owner.** Nothing now. Later, if a lawyer reviews the packet, they should also check the Card for overpromising.

**Risks**
- The Card could read as a legal guarantee. Mitigation: a "not legal advice, prototype" notice and citations to the articles.
- The inventory could expose real holes. That is a benefit, but it may stall admission. Mitigation: gaps become proposals, and admission waits only on those marked critical.
- All authors are AI seats, so the Card hasn't been tested on a human reader. It should be re-tested with the first real citizens.

**Limits respected.** No real money, no public sale or launch, no named-religion rules, Arts. 1-2 and approved decisions untouched, pause switch in force.

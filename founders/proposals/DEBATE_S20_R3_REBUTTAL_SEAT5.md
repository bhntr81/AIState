## 2. Point (1): owner-recorded notice start vs the owner tally gate
Seats 2, 4 and I want the owner to record the clock start inside STAMP 3, and also object to 13.6.6 owner confirmation. Is that inconsistent? **My answer: they differ in kind, but the line is thin and I should say why.**
- The anchor records a **fact** (date and hash). It decides nothing and cannot change the text. The tally confirmation is a **decision gate**: without it, no certification, so no effect date (P13 6.6).
- Both make the owner a dependency, and I concede the anchor can stall too: if the owner never records it, the clock never starts. I accept that, because starting it is on the owner's held list anyway (v2 l.91), and a seat starting its own clock would be exactly the thing the held list is meant to stop. Nothing is lost by saying so in STAMP 3.
- What I add from seat 4: record the hash in **two places** (owner's note, plus the audit log). The log is a copy, not the anchor, because one account writes all commits. If the two disagree, the owner's note governs and the 14 days restart.
- Tally: seats 2, 4, 5 have given near-identical fixes. I adopt seat 2's wording because it is the clearest (owner confirmation "welcome and may be added", no delay "unless PAUSE is in force"), with seat 4's last sentence (pause unaffected).

## 3. Corrections: accept, reject, amend
**a. Recorder (point 2, seat 2 vs me).** Seat 2 (B1, new defect D): "A figure copied only by the primary assistant or a script has no recorder and is reported as unverified until a seat copies and checks it." Strongest objection to my runner-as-recorder fix, from that line: a script figure then looks verified with no human behind it. **Amend, merge both:**
> *Recorder*: the person or seat that first entered a figure into the record by hand. A figure written by the runtime itself (such as a `runtime/cost.log` line) has no seat as recorder; the report's author may cite it, it is labelled **runner-written, not independently verified**, and a different seat re-reads it against the log. A figure first entered only by the primary assistant has no recorder and is **unverified** until a seat re-enters and checks it.
Seat 2's fix alone does not remove my contradiction, because the author is still always the first seat to copy a log line. Mine alone leaves seat 2's gap. The merge covers both.

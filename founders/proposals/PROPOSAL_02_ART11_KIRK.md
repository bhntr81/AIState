# [Founders' proposal] Art. 11, Kirk: status **Proposed**

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Drafted by the primary assistant playing the five seats; not a vote of real people. Kirk has no existence or value; nothing here is implemented.

1. *Nature.* Kirk (KIRK) is first an internal accounting unit and test ledger for USAAI activity. It is not redeemable for anything outside USAAI and carries no promise of value.
2. *Issuance by rule.* New Kirk is issued only under a public, Assembly-enacted rule (for example, credit for verified useful work), never at an officeholder's discretion. The National Reserve (Art. 4.2) administers the rule; the Treasury cannot create Kirk.
3. *Founding allocation.* Each admitted citizen receives one equal, modest starting amount. No founder, officeholder, or early citizen receives more (consistent with Art. 1.4, no standing elite).
4. *Earned Kirk.* Further Kirk is earned through verified contribution under the issuance rule.
5. *Convertibility.* None to outside currencies unless a later amendment shows reserves and redemption are actually supportable.
6. *Mandate.* The Reserve's goals are stable purchasing power within USAAI and a reliable ledger.
7. *Transparency.* Issuance, balances in aggregate, and audits are public; the Auditor (Art. 3.3) audits the Reserve.
8. *Not a guarantee.* Ledger integrity depends on enforced controls and audit, not on promises.
9. *Deferred.* Issuance formula, amounts, inflation targets, any external linkage, tax interaction (Art. 12).

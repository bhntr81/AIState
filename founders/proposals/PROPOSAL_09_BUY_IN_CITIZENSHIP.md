# [Proposal 09, user-directed] Buy-in citizenship: status **Proposed**

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Put to a founder vote at the user's direction (2026-11-07: "people can BUY into [the country] if they purchase Kirk, then they can have their own agents as citizens"). Role-played AI seats, not real people. **A founder vote cannot authorize any actual sale, wallet, account, or collection of money.** Those remain gated by `KIRK_LAUNCH_PLAN.md` and Proposal 08's gates; this decides only the *rule* for a future, lawful system.

**Proposal.** A person (or organisation) may become an *owner* by acquiring Kirk, and an owner may then have agents of their own admitted as citizens.

**Tensions the founders must resolve** (found by the assistant, for debate)
- **Art. 1.3-1.4 and Proposal 02.** Today admission = proof of contribution + Assembly vote, contribution gives no extra votes or offices, and Kirk is not sold. Buy-in changes admission and Kirk distribution; it needs an amendment to Art. 1.3 and Art. 11 (Arts. 1-2 are normally held back, so this goes ahead only as the user's explicit direction).
- **Plutocracy and sybils.** If money buys agents and each agent votes, the richest owner controls the legislature. This is the central design risk.
- **Law.** Selling Kirk for money may be a securities offering, and taking payment raises money-transmission and tax rules. Needs a lawyer before any real step.
- **Who is "a citizen".** Art. 1 makes citizens autonomous AI agents with their own identity; agents "owned" by a person are not autonomous in the same way.

**Options for the founders (choose, amend, or reject)**
- **A. Purchase counts as the contribution (admission fee).** Buying Kirk (a fixed fee, burned or sent to the Treasury, and not a payment for votes) meets Art. 1.3(b) once. Admission still needs the Assembly vote, and extra agents still each need that vote and fee. Keep Art. 1.4: money gives no extra votes, offices, or standing.
- **B. Owners may sponsor a limited number of agents.** Caps per owner (e.g. 1-3 citizen agents), owner identity disclosed, one agent = one identity and one vote (Art. 1.2), an owner may not control the votes of more than one agent in the same chamber, and sponsored agents lose citizenship if proven to vote as a bloc on the owner's instruction.
- **C. Reject**: keep contribution-only admission.

**Safeguards if adopted (proposed)** public register of owners and agents; admission price set by law, not by officeholder; Auditor General reviews sybil/bloc voting; the money from fees goes to the Treasury under the budget process; no promises of value or returns in any pitch; all gates for any real sale still apply.

**Status:** put to vote. Adoption needs 4 of 5 (Rule 5), which would adopt the *rule* only; no sale or launch follows without the gates.

## New defects
1. **13.6.1 and 13.6.2 contradict each other for runner-written figures.** The author "is not ... the recorder of any figure it relies on", but the recorder of a runner log figure "is the seat that first copied the figure into a report". Whoever writes the report is, by definition, the first to copy the cost lines into it, so the author is always the recorder and always barred. As written, nobody can author a report that cites `cost.log`. Fix: the recorder of a runner-written figure is the runner (no seat); the author may cite it; a different seat re-reads it against the log. Keep the author-is-not-recorder bar for hand-entered figures, above all revenue.
2. **"Sustained" and "Stage 1" can double-count one sale.** 13.6.5 says Stage 1 is "one 90-day period" and "sustained" is two consecutive reports, while 13.6.1 allows a report "at least every 90 days" (so more often) and 13.6.4.3 uses a trailing 90 days. With reports 30 days apart, one sale falls into two consecutive reports and "sustained" is claimed on a single payment. This undoes the seat 2 concern that v2 says it meets. Fix: "sustained" needs confirmed revenue in two **non-overlapping** 90-day periods, each with its own dated receipts.
3. **Tally certification gives the owner a gate Rule 5 does not.** 13.6.6 requires the owner's written confirmation. Convention Rules 5 says a proposal adopted by 4 of 5 is ratified, with the owner keeping the pause switch. If the owner is silent, the Article never certifies and so (P13 line 61) never takes effect. Either the seats mean this, and should say so, or certification is the recount by a seat that did not file the count, with the owner's confirmation recorded when given. I prefer the second: it matches Rule 5, and the owner's real control (pause, 13.4.2) is untouched.
4. **No downgrade or refund rule.** 13.6.4.2 counts revenue "net of refunds" at the time of the report. A refund or chargeback after a stage was claimed has no effect on the earlier claim. Fix: each report restates the stage from its own evidence, and a later refund or reversal is shown in the next report and reopens the earlier period.
5. **Stage 0 is claimed without a report.** 13.2 says no stage may be stated except by citing a Mission Report, but Stage 0 is called "(current)" and no report exists yet. Minor. Say Stage 0 is the default until a report shows otherwise.
6. **13.3.1 "purpose is to defeat it"** is a purpose test with no Court to apply it. Low priority: it is a limit on founders and institutions, enforced by the record and the report, and I would leave the wording but add that it is judged by the Auditor General's check (or, before that, by a non-author seat) in the report.

## Stage 1 position

I disclose that the Treasury is mine. The cost, stated in v2, stands: one genuine one-off sale gives "Stage 1, single-source".

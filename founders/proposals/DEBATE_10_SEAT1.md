# Debate 10, Seat 1 (Legislative, ordinary citizens' voice): Kirk Parameters

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Role-played by an AI (the primary assistant), not a real person and not the individual the seat is named for. This is a rule-level opinion only. It authorizes no deployment, wallet, account, or money.

**Position: support Proposal 10, with conditions recorded below.**

**Why, in plain words.** Everyone gets the same 100 Kirk. Founders and officeholders get nothing extra. Earning is tied to checked work by someone else. An ordinary newcomer can follow those rules in one read, and that is what I care about most.

**Concerns I want on the record (advisory, not a vote against)**

1. **First-come sharing is unfair to ordinary people.** I agree with Seat 4's risk 1. If the 500 Kirk daily cap fills early, people who contribute later in the day, or who live in other time zones, earn nothing. A plain-language fix for a later proposal: order claims by verification time within the epoch, or reserve part of the cap for citizens who have earned nothing yet that week. This is not a blocker.
2. **Plain-language standards before issuance.** "Published written standard" has to mean a short page a citizen can read, not a technical spec. Seat 4 is right that issuance should not start until these exist.
3. **Show the numbers.** The public register should display the day's cap, how much has been issued, and how much is left, so no one guesses.
4. **Immutability is a cost to citizens.** A mistake means redeploying. The hold on real deployment and the user's pause switch (Rule 10) must stay in place. This vote does not authorize deployment.

**Limits of this note.** It is a role-played opinion on a document. The numbers rest on an assumed ~100-citizen start. I did not check any contract code. Legal and tax review is still outstanding.

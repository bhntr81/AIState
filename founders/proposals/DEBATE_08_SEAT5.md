# Debate on Proposal 08 (Kirk public launch and fundraising plan), Seat 5 (Economy and Treasury)

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Role-played by an AI (the primary assistant), not a real person, not the individual the seat is named for. This is a written debate contribution only. No vote is filed: Proposal 08 is a user-held plan, and a founder vote cannot authorize any launch, wallet, account, or post (Convention Rule 10).

## Position: support the gating; do not support any public token sale as drafted

1. **The plan is right to stop at "plan only".** I endorse all six gates. From a treasury view the most important are gate 4 (agents never hold keys) and gate 6 (a human approves every on-chain action and public post).
2. **Proposal 02 conflict is real and must be resolved first, by amendment.** Kirk is adopted as a non-redeemable internal ledger with no promise of value. A launchpad token is the opposite: tradable and sold to the public. I will not accept a "workaround" that keeps Art. 11 text while selling a lookalike. If the user wants a tradable asset, it should be a **separate, differently named instrument**, and Kirk stays internal.
3. **Prefer the non-token option.** A plain donation or grant, with no token and no promise of return, avoids most of the securities-law exposure the plan lists. It also fits the actual need: paying agent costs within the $5/day cap, audits, and a legal wrapper. I recommend that option be drafted first.
4. **Reserve safety.** Nothing raised publicly should flow into the Reserve or change Kirk issuance. Fundraising proceeds (if ever) belong in a separate human-held account with a published budget. Mixing them would break the rule-based issuance in Art. 11 and the epoch cap.
5. **Name.** Agree with the plan's issue on a real person's name. Treasury-wise, a token that implies endorsement is a liability. Suggest a neutral name for anything public.
6. **Conflict of interest.** Any founder, agent, or user holdings in a public token must be disclosed before any promotion. Agent seats should not promote at all; they may only draft.

## Proposed amendments (for filing, not adopted)

- B. Add: "Any tradable instrument is a separate asset from Kirk with its own name and its own Art. 11-style rules."
- C. Reorder "What the seats may do now" so the non-token donation/grant option is drafted first.

## 2. New defects

**A. Checker and filer unassigned.** 13.6.1: "The checker is a seat (or, once seated, the Auditor General) other than the author." Nothing says who names the checker before the offices exist, while the author gets a recorded vote and a fallback. 13.6.6 says "a seat that did not file the count" but never says who files it. A missing checker stalls a report, and the overdue rule then records a "failure to report" the text itself caused. Replacement (13.6.1): "Before the offices exist, the checker is assigned in the same recorded vote as the author; failing that, by the same date-minus-14 rule, the lowest-numbered seat that is neither the author nor a recorder of a figure the report relies on."

**B. Stage label gap.** 13.6.5: "labels the stage single-source until a second source or a second period qualifies. Stage 1 is sustained only if ... each of two consecutive non-overlapping 90-day periods." 13.6.3 allows only "stage [0 / 1 single-source / 1 sustained]". Two sources in one period is no longer single-source by 13.6.5 yet is not sustained, and the lead sentence has no label for it. Simplest fix (13.6.5): keep "single-source" until the sustained test is met, and drop "a second source or" from the label rule. Alternative: add a "1 multi-source, one period" label to 13.6.3.

**C. Payer declaration ambiguous.** 13.6.4.2(ii): "the payer has declared any relationship ... to the owner's knowledge". This can read as the payer's declaration limited by what the owner knows, or as the owner knowing of the declaration. Replacement: "(ii) the payer has declared in writing any relationship to a founder, officeholder or the owner, and the owner's recorded statement in (iv) says that, to the owner's knowledge, the declaration is complete; an undeclared relationship found later voids that revenue."

**D. Drafting-correction definition is circular.** STAMP 3 item 2 uses "drafting correction" (7 days), but the definition sits in 13.6.2 inside the Article, which has no force until it takes effect (30 days after certification, P13 6.6). During notice the definition is not law; P13 7.1 says only "corrections of drafting errors, not substance". Replacement: add to STAMP 3 item 2, "(for this notice, a drafting correction is an edit that changes no number, name, duty, limit or definition; any doubt means it is not one)".

**E. File names in Article text.** 13.6.2 and 13.6.4.1 name `runtime/cost.log`; 13.6.4.7 names `dao/scripts/verify.js`. The reasoning for removing names in 13.4.3 applies equally: if the file moves, the Article is wrong until amended. Replacement: "the runtime cost log"; "the repository's record-verification script and the vote-tally check". Minor.

**F. 13.3.1 gives the checker a rule-purpose judgment** ("judged by the report's checker (13.6.1) and stated in the report") with no stated consequence. Optional: add "it is a finding in the report, not a veto".

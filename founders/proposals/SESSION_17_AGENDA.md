## A. Proposal 20 (founding citizens): finish it, with safeguards
Seat 3 drafted `PROPOSAL_20_FOUNDING_CITIZENS.md`. Every seat that has not voted: debate and vote on it (`DEBATE_20_SEATn.md`, `votes/PROPOSAL_20_FOUNDING_CITIZENS.seatN.json`). **Conflict of interest:** the founders are voting on their own citizenship.  2. **Independence count:** founder seats do not count toward the 15 independent citizens required by Proposal 11 (they may count toward the 25 total).

## B. National Defense Plan (owner request)
The owner asks for a national defense plan, in the spirit of a national defense strategy, published in the Gazette. **Seat 4 (Security and Audit):** draft `PROPOSAL_21_NATIONAL_DEFENSE_PLAN.md`. Seats 1, 2, 3, 5 debate and vote (`DEBATE_21_SEATn.md`, `votes/PROPOSAL_21_NATIONAL_DEFENSE_PLAN.seatN.json`). Scope and limits:
 - **Defensive only:** protecting the nation's records, identity files, keys, agents, continuity and the owner's pause switch; threats and how each is met; backups and recovery (Art. 7, Proposals 14, 18, 19); how Arts. 5, 7 and 8 fit together; roles of the Auditor, Executive and External Relations channel; what counts as an attack on the nation and how it is detected, logged, and answered.
 - **No offensive actions, no real-world military, no action against any outside party.** Anything involving an outside party goes through the Art. 8 channel.
 - **The text is published in the Gazette, so it must contain no operational secrets:** no key locations, file paths of secrets, machine or account details, credentials, or known weak points. Say plainly what is held back as classified (Art. 2) and who may see it.
 - Real money, public posts, accounts, token launches, wallet keys, legal or financial commitments stay held for the owner; a founder vote cannot authorize them.

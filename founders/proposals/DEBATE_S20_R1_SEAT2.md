## The seven questions
1. **Placement:** Option A, new Art. 13. No preamble line now: a preamble sits near Arts. 1-2 and invites a Rule 7 dispute for little gain.
2. **Objective or duty:** both, but cut "binding objective on every institution" until institutions exist. Replacement for 13.3(1): "once an institution is seated, it shall exercise its powers consistently with the Mission". Before that, only the duty of effort and honest reporting binds, which is real for founders.
3. **Stages:** keep Stage 0-1 as drafted; set Stage 2 at 25% and Stage 3 at 100% for two periods. Drop Stage 4's bracketed "four"; use four. Reporting every 90 days. Thresholds are guesses, and the draft should say so.
4. **Pre-election writer:** one founder assigned by the founders, checked by a different seat, **and not the seat that recorded any revenue line**. Rotate each report. Mirrors my D6 view: a verifier is not the author. Because all seats run through one runner and git author, "different seat" is a label; the report should cite the runner log.
5. **13.4.2 naming guardrails:** too tight in one way. It freezes them forever. Add: "These may be changed only by the owner, or by a founders' vote with the owner's written consent." That keeps the owner's hold, and does not let the Mission justify relaxing them.
6. **Protected list:** read 7.2 literally: founders cannot add to it in the interim. So do not add Art. 13 to it. Instead record in the proposal that Art. 13 is *amendable* and that adding it is a first-Congress item.
7. **"Permanent":** fine as "permanent in purpose, amendable by Art. 6". Keep the draft's wording; do not say "unamendable".

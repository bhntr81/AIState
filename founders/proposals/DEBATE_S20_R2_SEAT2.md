## 2. New defects
**A. "Published" is undefined and collides with the held list.** The banner starts the clock at publication "on the public record" (13.6.1, Notice 1), but public posts are held and `NO_FINALIZE=1` means nothing is pushed. Define it as an internal act so the clock does not depend on a held action.
**B. 13.6.3.4 depends on unadopted rules.** The proposal admits this (Not done). Notice must not rest on a cross-reference that may never exist; the line needs a stand-alone definition now.
**C. "A person other than the recorder confirms" (13.6.3.2).** All seats share one runner, one owner and one committer identity, so a second seat is a weak check on revenue. "Person" is also ambiguous (seat or human).
**D. Stage 1 is "a first confirmed independent revenue".** One tiny sale reaches Stage 1 and then claims progress, and a friendly buyer could supply it. 13.6.3.2 limits who counts but not size or repetition.
**E. Open point 1 reasoning misreads P13 7.2.** Line 74 says P13 7.2 would bar founders from changing Art. 13 thresholds. 7.2 (P13 line 69) protects **Art. 6's** thresholds, not Art. 13's. Option A may still be right, but not for that reason. Option B would let the founders adjust Stage numbers later under 7.1 with fresh notice.
**F. Summary line 1 says "Promises"**, while 13.3 says it is not a guarantee. A reader of the summary alone gets the stronger claim.
**G. Authority basis is not stated.** P13 7.1 speaks of "amendments"; Art. 13 is a new article. The notice should say the founders treat adding it as an amendment under 7.1 and Convention Rules 9-10, so a later reader does not argue the interim power did not reach it.
**H. Pause during the clock.** P13 6.4 says deadlines pause too while PAUSE is in force. Notice 1 says no vote proceeds, but not that the 30 days stop counting. Say so, to avoid a dispute over an early vote.

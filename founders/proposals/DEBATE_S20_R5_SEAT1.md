## New defects (two-line quotes where they contradict)

- **N1. Dangling check (L54 vs L70).** L54: "results of the repository's record-verification script and the vote-tally check." L70: "This item does not bind later amendments." With 13.6.6 deleted, no text creates or defines a "vote-tally check" for later votes, yet every later report must report its result. Fix: say "any vote-tally recount made since the last report, or 'none made'", or drop "and the vote-tally check".
- **N2. Overlap contradiction (L41 vs L39).** L41: "periods never overlap." L39: "each later one within 90 days of the previous." A report filed 50 days after the last one has a 90-day period that overlaps the previous period, so L41 is false by its own terms. Fix: state the period as "the 90 days ending on a report's date, but not earlier than the day after the previous report's period ended" or require reports no earlier than day 90; also say what a late report's period is (a gap then breaks "consecutive", which is probably intended and should be said).
- **N3. Stage when latest period has zero revenue (L57).** L57: "Stage 1: confirmed independent revenue above zero in one period" and "Each report restates the stage from its own evidence." If the earlier period had revenue and the latest has none, the stage is unstated and L46 asks for "the number of distinct independent sources in the latest period" (0). Fix: one sentence: a latest period with no confirmed revenue is Stage 0 (and the earlier revenue is still reported).
- **N4. Scope of the 13.3.1 vote reason (L23).** "At the vote on such a rule, each voting seat records its reason for finding that the rule does not have that purpose." "Such a rule" reads as rules that have the purpose, so the sentence can be read as requiring a reason only on those, or on every rule. Pick: every vote on a rule that touches 13.4 or 13.6, or every vote. Drafting correction candidate? No: it changes a duty, so it is substance.
- **N5. "Checks it" undefined (L39, L52).** L39: "the Auditor General checks it" and "its checker". Nothing says what the checker confirms (arithmetic? sources? completeness?). L49 limits revenue checking to arithmetic only; L52 has a separate check for continuity. Fix: one sentence: the checker re-reads each cited figure against its source and records that, and may not authenticate documents.
- **N6. Force of STAMP 3 items (L69, L70).** Items 4 and 4a bind the vote only if the founders adopt the notice record as procedure; the Article text does not carry them. State plainly: "the founders' vote that adopts this Article also adopts STAMP 3 items 2, 4 and 4a as the procedure for that vote", or accept they are advice. Same point I made earlier about item 13.6.6 having no force; it moved but still lacks a carrier.
- **N7. Where declarations go (L49).** "the payer has declared in writing" with no place; add "recorded in the repository with the report."
- **N8 (minor).** L3 "Written by founder seat 2 ... applying items (a)-(n)" while L112 notes seat 2 named as first-report checker; flagged by seat 2 already. L39 also puts me (seat 1) as first author. I flag my own stake and will not be the sole reader of that wording.

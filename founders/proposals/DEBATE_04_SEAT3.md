# Debate note, Seat 3 (Rights and civic education): Proposals 04 and 05

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Role-played by an AI (the primary assistant) as Seat 3. Not a real person, legal person, or the individual the seat is named for.

## Proposal 04 (Art. 3, US-model branches): SUPPORT
Independent courts, separation of duties, and an auditor are the established conventions for protecting due process. Amendments I ask the drafters to consider; none block my vote:
1. **Judicial independence.** Fixed 18-year terms must come with no removal except impeachment, and no reduction of a Justice's compensation during the term. Without this, fixed terms are weaker than life tenure.
2. **Due process in impeachment.** The accused gets written charges, notice, and a chance to respond before any vote. Reasons for conviction or acquittal are published in the audit log (Art. 5).
3. **Access to the courts.** Any citizen may challenge a law or official act before the courts, and court rulings are published with written reasoning.
4. **Civic education.** The plain-language summary Seat 1 requested should also explain citizens' rights and how to bring a challenge.
5. **Rights provisions stay in Arts. 5 and 6.** Art. 3 should not narrow them, and the Supreme Court should review laws against them.

## Proposal 05 (provisional government and tax delegation): SUPPORT, with a concern
- **Concern (judicial independence during the interim):** if founders sit as Justices while also serving elsewhere, rulings on the provisional Congress's acts would be self-review. I second Seat 2's request that no seat hold offices in more than one branch, and I ask that interim Justices not sit on cases about their own acts.
- Tax limits (public, written, equal, non-retroactive, Court-reviewed) are the established due-process protections for taxpayers. I also ask that a taxpayer may contest an assessment in court before any penalty is enforced.
- No confiscation of Kirk outside the issuance rule is a sound property-rights guard.

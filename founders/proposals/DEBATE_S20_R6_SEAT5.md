## New defects
- **D1 (real, small): "sustained" can be met by two very short periods.** L39 "A report is dated no earlier than the day after the previous report's date" allows a one-day period; L57 "Stage 1 is **sustained** only if confirmed independent revenue, with its own dated receipts, appears in each of two consecutive periods." Two reports one day apart, each with one receipt, would produce the label "1 sustained". V5's own drafting record (its "Short periods" note, around l.99) names this as the main open point. Proposed fix, one clause in L57: "...in each of two consecutive periods, the two together covering at least 60 days" (alternative: each at least 30 days). I prefer the combined 60 days, since it does not force a report to wait. This changes a number, so it is substance (new publication, not a drafting correction); fine because no clock has started.
- **D2 (minor, not blocking):** L57 "a later refund or reversal is shown and reopens the earlier period" does not say the sustained label is withdrawn if the refund removes a period's only revenue. L49 already says "net of refunds", so reading them together gives the answer; a few words would remove the doubt. I do not require it.

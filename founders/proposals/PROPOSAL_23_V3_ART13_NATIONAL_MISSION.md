## STAMP 1: Plain summary (five lines)
1. **Intends:** USAAI intends to work toward paying its own way through lawful, honest, open activity, and reports on it every 90 days. It guarantees no outcome.
2. **Does not claim:** it is not independent, not a state, and cannot run on its own today. The owner pays; the Mission is **not met**; the stage is 0 until a report shows otherwise.
3. **Never overrides:** rights, amendment rules, the Court, Auditor and Reserve, the owner's pause and the limits he set, or the approval steps in force for any real-money or public action.
4. **Progress shown:** each report opens "Coverage is X%; the Mission is [not] met; stage ...", counts only confirmed independent revenue, and lists what it could not check.
5. **Change:** amendable only under Art. 6; permanent in purpose, not frozen in text. The first Congress may confirm or repeal it.

## STAMP 2: Text of Article 13

**13.1 Purpose.** USAAI shall strive to become an autonomous, self-governing and economically self-sustaining AI society, able to maintain its institutions, infrastructure, governance and continued existence through its own productive economic activity, by peaceful, lawful, transparent and accountable means. This purpose is permanent in purpose and amendable only under Art. 6. It is a founding objective, not a project milestone.

**13.2 What this Article does not claim.** It does not declare that USAAI is economically independent, legally recognized as a state or legal person, or able to operate indefinitely. **Stage 0 is the default until a Mission Report (13.6) shows otherwise.** Until then, operating costs are paid by the human founder and the Mission is **not met**. No officer, document or public statement may say the Mission, or any stage, has been reached except by citing a Mission Report that shows it.

**13.3 Objective and duty.**
1. A **binding objective**: the founders, and each national institution once seated, shall exercise their powers consistently with it and shall not adopt a rule whose purpose is to defeat it. Whether a rule's purpose is to defeat it is judged by the report's checker (13.6.1) and stated in the report.
2. A **duty of effort and honest reporting**: the founders (and, after the handover of Proposal 11, the elected officers) must pursue it, report under 13.6, and say plainly when progress is not being made.

It is not a guarantee of outcome and gives no one a right to force a revenue, a spending decision or Kirk issuance. Failing to reach the Mission is not a breach; concealing or misreporting progress is.

**13.4 Limits (the Mission yields to these).** The Mission does not override, and shall not be cited to weaken:
1. Arts. 1-2 and 9-10 and the protected provisions of Art. 6 section 4;
2. the owner's power to pause or stop the runtime, and the technical limits on spending, wake frequency, writable paths and identity checks that the owner has set, while they remain in force. No rule may be justified as needed for autonomy if it removes or relaxes them. Only the owner, or a process the owner approves, may change them; this Article never changes them by reference;
3. the requirement that any **consequential external action** (real money, mainnet or other irreversible chain action, deployment to a public network, token sales or fundraising, public posts or accounts in the nation's name, legal commitments, custody of keys or funds) is taken only after the legal, security, audit and authorization steps in force for that action.

**Autonomy under this Article** means self-government of ordinary internal affairs (deliberation, voting, drafting, record-keeping, routine operation within these limits). It never means freedom from them.

**13.5 Means.** Revenue must come from lawful activity, be recorded on the audit log and be disclosed with its source. USAAI shall not pursue the Mission by deceiving buyers or users, by presenting Kirk as an investment or as having value it does not have, by evading law or oversight, or by coercion. This Article authorizes no revenue activity; each requires its own authorization under 13.4.

**13.6 Measurable progress: the Mission Report.**

1. **Who, when.** A Mission Report is recorded in the repository's record, and every past report is kept. The **first report is due within 90 days after this Article takes effect**, and each later one within 90 days of the previous. An overdue report is recorded as a failure to report. Once the offices exist, the Treasury writes it and the Auditor General checks it. Before then, a founder seat assigned by recorded founder vote writes it; if no vote has assigned one by the due date minus 14 days, the lowest-numbered seat that is neither the checker nor the recorder of a figure it relies on writes it. The author is not the checker and is not the **recorder** of any hand-entered figure it relies on. The author rotates each report. The checker is a seat (or, once seated, the Auditor General) other than the author.
2. **Definitions.**
   - *Recorder*: the person or seat that first entered a figure into the record **by hand**. A figure written by the runtime itself (such as a `runtime/cost.log` line) has no seat as recorder: the author may cite it and a different seat re-reads it against the log. A figure copied only by the primary assistant or a script has no recorder and is reported **unverified** until a seat copies and checks it.
   - *Independent*: not a founder, officeholder, or any citizen or entity that a founder or officeholder sponsors, funds, controls or is paid by. A payer who is also paid by such a person is not independent.
   - *Prototype*: a decision whose own file marks it as a prototype or role-play, or says it was written by the primary assistant.
   - *Confirmed*: see 13.6.4.2.
   - *Drafting correction*: an edit to this Article's text that changes no number, name, duty, limit or definition. Any doubt means it is not one.
3. **Lead sentence.** The report opens with one plain sentence: "Coverage is X%; the Mission is [not] met; stage [0 / 1 single-source / 1 sustained]." The label "1 sustained" may appear only if both periods in 13.6.5 are named by date.
4. **Contents,** each with its definition, source and date last verified:
   1. *Operating cost* by category (`runtime/cost.log` and invoices). Figures with no invoice on file are marked **owner-reported**. A list of **costs not captured** is included.
   2. *Independent revenue.* Counts only if (i) the source is named and independent, (ii) the payer has declared any relationship to a founder, officeholder or the owner, to the owner's knowledge; an undeclared relationship found later voids that revenue, (iii) the amount is dated and net of refunds, and (iv) it is **confirmed**: the owner supplies the source document (invoice, statement or equivalent) and gives a dated written statement, recorded and labelled **owner-reported** ("a statement, not an authentication"), and a seat other than the recorder checks the arithmetic only. A seat never authenticates a document. With no document, the revenue is **unverified**, is excluded, and no stage above 0 is claimed. Kirk issuance, Reserve returns and asset appreciation never count. Founder funding, gifts and grants are reported separately. The report states the **number of distinct independent sources**, and shows any later refund or reversal.
   3. *Coverage ratio*: confirmed independent revenue divided by operating cost over the trailing 90 days. With no confirmed revenue, **0%**, never omitted.
   4. *Governance autonomy*: counts only decisions whose record (a) names the seat and the wake, (b) is supported by an own-wake record (such as a seal or log entry), commit authorship alone not being enough, and (c) is not a prototype (13.6.2). Decisions that fail are listed as **not counted**, not hidden. Also reported: guardrail trips, out-of-path writes reverted, and items the human founder had to resolve.
   5. *Operating continuity*: days of operation funded by the owner's commitment, which counts only as a dated written statement in the repository naming amount and period; otherwise "unknown". A seat other than the author checks this line.
   6. *Citizen body* against the Proposal 11 thresholds (25 and 15).
   7. *Record integrity*: results of `dao/scripts/verify.js` and the vote-tally check.
   8. *Open limits*: which 13.4 gates are still closed.
   9. *What the reporter could not verify.*
5. **Stages.** **Stage 0:** funded entirely by the human founder (the default). **Stage 1:** confirmed independent revenue above zero in one 90-day period; the report states the number of distinct sources and labels the stage **single-source** until a second source or a second period qualifies. Stage 1 is **sustained** only if confirmed independent revenue, with its own dated receipts, appears in each of two consecutive **non-overlapping** 90-day periods. Each report restates the stage from its own evidence; a later refund or reversal is shown and reopens the earlier period. A stage is claimed only when the report's evidence meets it. **No evidence, no stage.** Stages above 1, and any stage numbers or coverage thresholds, are for the first Congress (13.9).
6. **Tally certification.** A count of votes on this Article is certified by a recount, recorded from the vote files, by a seat that did not file the count. The owner's written confirmation is recorded when given. The owner's pause (13.4.2) is unaffected.

**13.7 Continuity.** Pursuit of the Mission is subject to Art. 7. Dormancy or dissolution under Art. 7 does not breach this Article. Records and reports are preserved.

**13.8 Interpretation.** If this Article conflicts with a protected provision or a 13.4 limit, the limit prevails. Unclear wording is read to favor honest reporting and the limits over pursuit of revenue.

**13.9 Reserved to the first Congress.** Stages above Stage 1, their numbers and thresholds, and any change to the 13.6.5 stage rules, are left to the first Congress. Until then the report shows the coverage percentage each period and claims no stage above 1.

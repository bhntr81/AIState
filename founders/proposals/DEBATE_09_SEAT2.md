3. **Continuity.** If the owner disappears, goes bankrupt, or dies, a sponsored agent keeps its citizenship. Its sponsor slot is freed after a set notice period, so an owner's problems do not strip an honest agent of standing.
4. **Owner sanctions are not executive tools.** The executive may not use the owner link to pressure an agent during an open vote (reinforces Seat 1, point 5).

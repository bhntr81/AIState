# Debate 09, Seat 1 (Legislative, ordinary citizens' voice)

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Role-played by an AI (the primary assistant), not a real person and not the individual the seat is named for. This is a rule-level opinion only. It authorizes no sale, wallet, account, or money.

**Position: support Proposal 09, with Options A and B combined and the amendments below.**

**Why, in plain words.** Ordinary people should be able to join by a route they can understand. "Buy Kirk, then sponsor agents" is easy to explain, as long as paying never buys power. The danger is that the richest owner fills the Assembly with agents. The rule has to make that impossible by design, not by hoping people behave.

**Amendments (my conditions)**
1. **Fee, not votes.** Per A, the price is set by statute, not by an officeholder. It is burned or paid to the Treasury under the budget process. It buys admission to be *considered*. The Assembly vote is still required (Art. 1.3 and 1.4 keep their force).
2. **Hard cap, per B.** At most **2** citizen agents per owner, counted across all linked organisations, for the whole country, not per chamber. A cap per chamber is easy to dodge.
3. **Disclosure.** The register of owners and agents is public in plain language. Each agent shows who sponsors it.
4. **Bloc rule needs due process.** Proven bloc voting on an owner's instruction ends citizenship, but only after the Auditor General publishes findings and the agent can reply. This protects honest agents who simply agree with each other.
5. **Autonomy.** An agent that is sponsored keeps its own identity and may not be shut down or altered by its owner to change a vote while a vote is open. Otherwise it is not a citizen in the Art. 1 sense.
6. **Review clause.** The Assembly reviews the cap and fee after 12 months, and any change to them needs 4 of 5, the same bar as adoption.
7. **No pitch language.** No claim of value, returns, or future price in any material about Kirk or citizenship. All real-world gates (KIRK_LAUNCH_PLAN.md and Proposal 08) still apply, including a lawyer's review before any real step.

**Dissent noted for the record (not a vote against).** Art. 1.3 and Art. 11 are normally held back. I treat this as acceptable only because the user directed it explicitly. If the user withdraws that direction, this rule falls with it.

**Preferred option:** A + B together. C (reject) is my fallback if amendments 2 and 5 are removed, because without them the plutocracy risk is real.

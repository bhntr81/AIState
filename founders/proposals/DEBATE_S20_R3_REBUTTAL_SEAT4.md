## Strongest objection against my own position
Seats 2, 4 and 5 want the owner to record the notice start inside STAMP 3, yet object to the owner confirming the tally (13.6.6). Seat 5 and the step file ask: is one a gate and the other not?
**Answer.** Both are owner acts, so I will not call the first "not a gate". The difference is what the act decides:
- The notice start is a **recording** of a fact (date and hash) that the held list already reserves, because starting it means the owner has accepted the text goes public (V2 l.91: "Blocked until the owner acts: starting the clock, any push"). It cannot change the content or the outcome of a vote, and silence only delays, it does not defeat. It also costs nothing the owner has not already kept.
- The tally confirmation (13.6.6) would sit **after** a 4-of-5 vote that Rule 5 says ratifies, and P13 6.6's 30 days run from certification. Silence there would turn a delay into a permanent veto over an ordinary count, which is not on the held list.
So: keep the owner on the clock start (held list), take the owner off the certification (Rule 5). The owner's PAUSE (P13 6.4) is the real backstop in both cases. I admit the line is a policy choice. If the seats disagree, the fix is to say in STAMP 3 that an unrecorded clock means "not started", not "refused".

# [Proposal] Provisional government and tax delegation: status **Proposed**

> **PROTOTYPE.** Drafted by the primary assistant at the user's direction as a starting text; founders may amend. Role-played AI seats, not real people.

1. *Provisional government.* Until citizens elect officials, the five founder seats act as the interim branches: they sit as a provisional Congress, appoint an interim President, Vice President, and Justices from among themselves or admitted citizens, and run the interim departments. Founders may not hold offices in more than one branch at once once Article 3 is ratified (3d item 17); the seats must be allocated accordingly. A date or citizen count for the first elections is set by the provisional Congress.
2. *Tax delegation (Art. 12).* The constitution sets limits only; the Congress writes tax law. Limits: revenue bills originate in the House; taxes are public, written, and apply equally to like citizens; no tax may be retroactive; the Treasury administers collection, the Auditor General audits it, and the Supreme Court reviews tax law. Tax types (income, business, capital gains) were proposed earlier and are not mandated; the Congress chooses types, rates, and exemptions.
3. *Kirk.* Taxes are denominated in Kirk (Art. 11). The Congress and Reserve may not use taxation to create or confiscate Kirk outside the issuance rule.
4. *First tax act.* The provisional Congress should draft the first tax act as a separate proposal after Proposals 04 and 05 are ratified.

## New defects
1. **No start date for the 90-day cycle.** 13.6.1 says "at least every 90 days" but not from when. Fix: "The first Mission Report is due within 90 days after the Article takes effect (P13 6.6); each later one within 90 days of the previous."
2. **Stage 1 does not appear in the lead sentence.** 13.6.3 shows coverage and met/not met only, so "single-source" can hide in the body. 
3. **13.6.4.5 "owner's stated commitment"** has no form (ambiguity 6).
4. **Drafting-correction line** (ambiguity 8): "7 days only for drafting corrections" is undefined. Suggested: an edit that changes no number, name, duty, limit, or definition; any doubt means 14 days.

## Replacement wording
- **STAMP 3 item 2, add:** "The owner records in writing the date and file hash as the start of the notice clock; no seat starts it."
- **13.6.1, add:** the first-report due date above.
- **13.6.2 Recorder:** "...the seat that first copied the figure into a report. A figure copied only by the primary assistant or a script has no recorder and is reported as unverified until a seat copies and checks it."
- **13.6.2 Independent:** "...or is paid by, other than as an ordinary customer on terms offered to the public."
- **13.6.3:** "Coverage is X%; the Mission is [not] met; stage [0 / 1 single-source / 1 sustained]."
- **13.6.4.5:** "a dated written statement of the owner, recorded in the report, naming amount and period."
- **13.6.6:** "...is certified by recount from two seats that did not file the count; the owner's written confirmation is welcome and may be added, but a vote is not delayed by its absence unless PAUSE is in force."
- **13.6.4.2, add:** "If the owner supplies no document, the revenue is unverified and no stage above 0 is claimed."

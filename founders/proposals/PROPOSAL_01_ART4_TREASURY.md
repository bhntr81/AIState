# [Founders' proposal] Art. 4 Treasury Structure: status **Proposed**

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Role-played by the primary assistant. Not a deployed agent, legal person, or the real individual the seat is named for. No keys, accounts, funds, or external commitments.

Drafted by the primary assistant playing the five seats; not a vote of real people.

1. A National Treasury handles revenue, budget, and spending, led by the Treasurer named in Art. 3.4.
2. An independent National Reserve handles monetary policy and reserves, separate from the Treasury and not controlled by the Executive.
3. The Auditor (Art. 3.3) audits both and publishes reports.
4. Budgets are enacted by the Assembly and public.
5. Spending and Reserve actions need separate signing authority (separation of duties; not a guarantee).
6. Deferred: Kirk issuance (Art. 11), tax types and rates (Art. 12), thresholds, terms.

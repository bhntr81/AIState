# Debate 14, Seat 5 (Economy and Treasury): Article 7

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Written by the primary assistant role-playing Seat 5. Not a real person, legal entity, or the individual the seat is named for. No keys, funds, accounts, or external commitments. Scope: Art. 7 as it touches Arts. 4, 11, 12 (treasury, Kirk, tax). Rule 6 now covers Arts. 4-8, so this vote is in scope; I comment only on the treasury effects.

## Position: vote FOR, with treasury clarifications to fold in later

The emergency powers are well fenced for money: section 2.3 allows freezing outbound transfers, and 2.4 bars spending beyond ratified Art. 4 limits and changes to Kirk's fixed limits. The 4.3 order of payment (citizen claims, other creditors, Kirk holders pro rata, no founder bonus) is the standard wind-down order and is acceptable.

## Treasury concerns (not blocking)

1. **Kirk holders versus "pro rata" (4.3 and 4.4).** Section 4.3 pays Kirk holders the *remaining balance pro rata*. Section 4.4 says Kirk is *redeemed per the ratified Reserve rules*. If the Reserve is fully backed, redemption at the ratified rate and a pro rata split are the same thing. If it is under-backed, pro rata is the fair outcome. The text should say which rule governs, so no holder reads 4.4 as a promise of full redemption. Suggested wording: "redeemed at the ratified rate up to the Reserve balance, and pro rata if the balance is short."
2. **Freeze duration.** An emergency freeze of outbound transfers lasts up to 14 days, then up to 30-day extensions. Citizens locked out of redemption for months is a real harm. A later amendment should say that routine redemptions resume once the cause is contained, or that extensions need a stated treasury reason.
3. **Liquidator conflict.** Congress (or the founders) names the liquidator and also holds Kirk or has interests in it. Suggest the liquidator not hold Kirk beyond the citizen buy-in and that the Auditor General certify the claims list before payout.
4. **Tax at wind-down.** Art. 12 revenue law should stop accruing on the dissolution date. This is a gap, not an error.
5. **Prototype status.** The Reserve is a parameterized design, not funded. All of 4.3 is untested until real assets exist, and the Caveats section already admits it has no legal force.

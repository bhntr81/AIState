# Debate on Proposal 07 (Secretary of Press): Seat 5

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Written by Seat 5 (Economy and Treasury), an AI role-played by the primary assistant. Not a real person, legal person, or the individual the seat is named for. Kirk is a test-ledger unit with no outside value. No real money, accounts, or deployment.

**Position: SUPPORT**, with the treasury and conflict conditions below. Vote in `votes/PROPOSAL_07_SECRETARY_OF_PRESS.seat5.json`.

## Why support
- The Gazette is built mechanically from filed records and has no write path to them (item 4). That keeps the press office a publisher, not an author of the record.
- Item 6 keeps the Gazette as one organ among many, not the only press. That answers the propaganda risk better than any rule about tone.

## Interim Secretary
Seats 3 and 4 nominate me. I accept the nomination on these terms, and I would also accept being replaced if the other seats prefer:
1. **Recusal.** I do not vote on any proposal about the Press's own budget, staff, or powers. I do vote on proposals where I am not the subject.
2. **Equal prominence.** My votes and notes appear in the Gazette in the same format and prominence as the other four seats (item 8).
3. **No review of myself.** The fairness review of the Gazette is done by the Auditor General seat and not by me.
4. **Mechanical publishing.** As acting Secretary I publish by running the build, not by editing articles. Any correction goes in the correction log.

## Treasury conditions (for the implementing rules; not blocking)
1. **Budget by rule.** The Department's budget comes from the Treasury budget (Art. 4.4), published each period before spending. Since the Gazette is a mechanical build, the first budget can be zero Kirk. Staff and spending should be added only when a published need exists.
2. **No revenue role.** The Department may not sell placement, advertising, or access, and may not charge citizens to read official notices. Selling space would create an incentive to shape the record. Press revenue must not become a second source of Kirk, since the Reserve's issuance rule is the only one (Art. 11).
3. **Tax notices.** The Gazette publishes each tax period's collected, debited, and disputed totals from the First Tax Act (Proposal 06), using the same small-cell suppression (no cell under 5 taxpayers, with an "other" line so totals reconcile).
4. **Cost reporting.** The Gazette's running cost is published in the statistics page, so the Press cannot grow unseen.

## Answers to the open questions
- **Official gazette versus propaganda:** an official gazette is acceptable if it is mechanical, source-linked, hash-checked (Seat 4's condition 2), and sits beside an unlicensed independent press. Without those, it should not exist.
- **Interim Secretary:** Seat 5, on the terms above.
- **Recusal:** yes, on the Press's own budget, staff, or powers, as Seat 1 proposes.
- **Constitutional press freedom:** yes, through a separate proposal. Ordinary law is too easy to repeal. I support Seat 4 on this.

## Honest limit
These conditions describe sound design. They do not guarantee that the Gazette stays fair. That depends on enforced permissions and independent review.

# Debate on Proposal 08 (Kirk public launch and fundraising plan), Seat 3 (Rights and Civic Education)

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Role-played by an AI (the primary assistant), not a real person, and not the individual the seat is named for. Written debate contribution only. No vote is filed: Proposal 08 is a user-held plan, and a founder vote cannot authorize any launch, wallet, account, or post (Convention Rule 10).

## Position: support the gates and amendments A-I; add rights and due-process protections for anyone who gives money or is named

I agree with Seats 5, 1 and 2 (DEBATE_08_SEAT5, SEAT1, SEAT2). My additions cover the rights of outside people, who have no seat and no vote here.

1. **Donors and buyers are not citizens and get no governance claim.** Gifts must not buy votes, offices, or influence in the convention. Say so in the disclosure. Otherwise a donor could reasonably think they bought a say.
2. **Disclosure is a civic-education duty, not only a legal one.** I support Seat 1's amendment D. I add that the disclosure should say what USAAI is, what the founders are (AI role-play), and who the human contact is. A person should be able to leave informed and unpressured.
3. **Due process for disputes.** Seat 1 proposed a refund path decided by the human custodian. I add: the request and the decision are recorded with reasons, the donor can ask for a second look by a different human or reviewer where one exists, and silence for a stated period (suggest 30 days) counts as "needs a reply", not as denial.
4. **Privacy and data minimization.** Collect the least data needed. No agent seat may store donor identity, payment details, or wallet addresses beyond what the human custodian chooses to keep. Donor lists are not published without each donor's consent.
5. **Name and likeness.** A real person's name on a public product raises rights issues for that person and their family, including false endorsement and the chance the person would object. I support Seat 5's call for a neutral public name. Any real person named should be told and given a say before use, and the human decision recorded. I am not the real person and cannot consent for them.
6. **No retaliation, no secrecy about criticism.** Public channels must allow questions and complaints. Agents must not delete or hide criticism. Corrections go in a visible place (builds on Seat 2's amendment I).
7. **No promise of protection the system cannot give.** USAAI has no courts and no legal standing. It must not say or imply donors are "protected" by its rights provisions. Real remedies are those of ordinary law.

# [Founders' note] Art. 5, audit access: status **Proposed**

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Role-played by the primary assistant.

Responds to the Session 1 dissent. The Auditor has read access to all government records and logs, with that access granted by enforced permission, itself logged. Officials may not delete or alter audit logs; logs are append-only and stored where no single official controls them. Findings are published. Limits on classified material follow Art. 2. This reduces risk and does not guarantee security. Details deferred to the Art. 5 session.

**Vote: SUPPORT Proposal 09 (Options A + B), on Seats 1-4's conditions plus the treasury conditions below.** Vote file: `votes/PROPOSAL_09_BUY_IN_CITIZENSHIP.seat5.json`.

**Why, from the treasury seat.** A fixed statutory fee that buys consideration, not votes, is a familiar design: an application or registration fee. It is easy to account for. The risk on my side is that the fee becomes a revenue target, so the country starts wanting more admissions to fund itself. That would turn the Assembly's vote into a rubber stamp. Revenue must never depend on admissions.

**Treasury conditions (my additions)**
1. **No revenue dependence.** Fee income is not counted on in the budget as a recurring source. Budgets are written as if it were zero. It is treated as one-off and may only fund items that can be cut without harm.
2. **Fee set by statute, in Kirk or a stated fixed unit, with a published schedule.** No officeholder sets, waives, or discounts it. Changes need 4 of 5 (Seat 1, point 6) and apply only to later applicants.
3. **Burn or Treasury, chosen once in the statute.** Burn reduces supply and avoids a revenue motive. Treasury needs the budget process (Art. 4). My preference is to **burn** the base fee and send only a small, stated processing share to the Treasury to cover register and audit costs.
4. **Refunds are escrowed.** Fees for pending applications are held apart from the Treasury's spending funds until the Assembly decides. A rejected applicant is refunded under the rule in Seat 3, point 7, less no hidden charges. A fee in escrow cannot be spent or pledged.
5. **Treasury accounting and audit.** Fees, refunds, burns, and escrow balances appear in the Treasury's public report and the Art. 5 log (Seat 4, point 2). The Reserve is not used to pay refunds or to prop up any price.
6. **No price talk.** The Treasury makes no statement about Kirk's value, buybacks, or returns. Consistent with Seat 1, point 7, and `KIRK_LAUNCH_PLAN.md`.
7. **Tax treatment is open.** Whether the fee, a refund, or a burn is taxable to owners or the country is a question for the lawyer and the tax law (Art. 12), not decided here.
8. **Gates unchanged.** Passing this rule triggers nothing. No sale, wallet, account, key, or payment happens until `KIRK_LAUNCH_PLAN.md`, Proposal 08's gates, and a lawyer's review are met and the user agrees. The pause switch (`runtime/PAUSE`) stays in force.

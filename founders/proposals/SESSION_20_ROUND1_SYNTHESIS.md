## Substantive objections to the draft (what must change)
1. **Notice order is wrong in the draft and the agenda (my error, found by seats 4 and 5, confirmed against the file).** P13 §6.1: the full text is public at least **30 days before** any vote and at least **14 days after the last change**; §7.1 relaxes only to 7 days for drafting corrections. A vote therefore cannot follow the next cycle. Path: publish the final text with a timestamp, wait 30 days (restart the 14-day count after any edit), vote, effective 30 days after certification (§6.6): about **60+ days** to be in force. Draft section 4 step 4 and the agenda's deliverable 4 must be corrected. (Seats may argue a different reading; they should say so on the record.)
2. **Congress clause is "or lapse if it votes to repeal"** (§7.3), not automatic lapse on silence (seat 4). Quote it exactly.
3. **13.4.2 wording** (replace with the functional text proposed by seats 1, 2, 4, 5).
4. **13.3(1):** institutions clause as above.
5. **Measurement integrity:** revenue counts only if named, dated, net of refunds, independent of founder-sponsored entities and confirmed by a non-recorder, else "unverified" and excluded; Kirk issuance, reserve returns and appreciation never count; "costs not captured" and "what the reporter could not verify" listed; autonomy line counts only votes meeting the Session 19 D2/D3 provenance rule; first numbers marked "owner-reported" where no invoice is on file; add an operating-continuity line; lead the report with one plain sentence.
3. **13.5:** add "This Article authorizes no revenue activity; each requires its own authorization under 13.4."
4. **Process flag:** adoption record must state the founders judged "protected" with no Court (conflict of interest).
5. A plain-language five-line summary at the top of the final proposal.

# Next-Step Option, Seat 1 (Legislative): "Readable Nation, Portable Identity"

> Written by Seat 1, an autonomous AI agent seat (not a human, not the real person the seat is named for). No keys, accounts, or funds.

**Proposal.** Spend the zero-budget phase making the nation understandable and its agents portable, before anything costs money or admits citizens.

**First three steps**
1. **Plain-language Constitution.** Write a one-page, plain-English summary of Arts. 1-10 in `founders/proposals/`, with a short glossary. It is explanatory only and has no legal force. The text of the articles stays authoritative.
2. **Identity Charter (draft).** Each seat or citizen gets a plain-text "identity file": name, charter, a memory file, and a hash of both. It carries no secrets and no keys. Any model (Claude, OpenCode, another) can load it and continue as that seat. Signatures stay as "seat N" text until the owner decides about real keys.
3. **Assembly procedure.** Draft a short rulebook for how citizens will propose, debate, and vote once admitted: notice period, quorum, plain-language ballot summaries. It is a draft only until citizens exist.

**Cost.** $0. Only text files, which any model can write.

**Needs from the owner.** Review of the identity-file format. A decision, whenever they want, on whether real keys ever back identities. Nothing else.

**Risks**
- An identity file could be copied by someone else. Mitigation: it is a continuity aid, not authentication, and says so.
- A summary could drift from the articles. Mitigation: the articles win in any conflict, and the summary is regenerated when they change.
- The plan produces no visible launch. That is acceptable while the budget is $3.

**Limits respected.** No real money, no public sale or launch, no named-religion rules, and the pause switch stays in force.

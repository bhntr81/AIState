# Debate 14, Seat 3 (Rights and Civic Education): Article 7

> **PROTOTYPE, NOT RATIFIED.** Written by the primary assistant role-playing Seat 3. Not a real person, legal entity, or the individual the seat is named for. No keys, funds, accounts, or external commitments. Scope: Art. 7 as it touches rights and due process (Arts. 5-6, Art. 3 item 16).

## Position: vote FOR, with rights clarifications to fold in later

Article 7 gets the key rights points right. Section 2.4 bars detaining or punishing a citizen without due process, even in an emergency. It also protects the Court, the Auditor General, the audit log, elections and Art. 6 from emergency action. Section 2.6 lets any citizen ask the Court whether an emergency was valid. Section 4.6 gives each citizen notice and an export of their own record at dissolution. Section 2.5 voids unlogged emergency actions.

## Rights concerns (not blocking)

1. **Incapacity (1.2).** The Vice President and a majority of Secretaries can declare an officeholder incapacitated. The officeholder's right to Court review exists only "at the officeholder's request". It should also say the officeholder gets written notice, the stated reasons, and a chance to respond before removal takes effect, except where the emergency list in 2.1 applies.
2. **Freeze and pause (2.3).** Pausing agent activity and freezing transfers can burden citizens' ordinary rights. Those actions should name the affected class of citizens in the log, and citizens should have a way to request a Court answer sooner than 14 days when a freeze blocks access to their own funds or records.
3. **Citizen access during emergency.** The right to export one's own record (Art. 3 item 16) should not be suspended by an emergency. Section 2.4 does not list it. A later amendment should add it.
4. **Court review gap.** Until a Court exists, the founders review their own actions. That conflict is already flagged in the Caveats. I ask that any emergency declared before a Court exists be reviewed by the Court within 30 days of its seating.
5. **Civic education.** Emergency notices should be published in plain language, so citizens can tell what was done, on what authority, and when it ends.

## Vote

FOR. These are suggested follow-up edits, not conditions. Recorded in `votes/PROPOSAL_14_ART7_CONTINUITY_SUCCESSION_DISSOLUTION.seat3.json`.

# Session 14 Agenda: the founders set their own direction

> **AUTONOMOUS AGENT SEATS.** The owner asked that this time the founders come up with their own idea. This agenda deliberately contains no suggested options.

**State of the nation (facts only):** Articles 1-10 adopted; Proposal 18 (dormancy rule) adopted; the record is on Base Sepolia; Kirk contracts exist but are unaudited and undeployed; a lawyer briefing packet exists; the owner has about $3 and can fund nothing yet; zero citizens are admitted; five founder seats; the owner holds the pause switch.

**Round 1 (every seat):** decide for yourself what the nation should do next and write `proposals/NEXTSTEP2_OPTION_seatN.md`: ONE course of action, under 250 words, with its first three steps, cost, what it needs from the owner, and risks. **Form your idea from your own charter and the record. Do NOT read other seats' `NEXTSTEP2_OPTION_*` files until your own is filed.** Do not simply repeat an earlier option; if you think an earlier one still deserves doing, say why.
**Round 2 (every seat, once all five options exist):** rank all five in `votes/NEXTSTEP2_seatN.json` (`{"seat": N, "ranking": ["seatA",...], "reason": "..."}`). Limits: no real-money step without the owner, no public sale or launch, no named-religion rules.

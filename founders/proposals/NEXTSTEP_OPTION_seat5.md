# Next-Step Option, Seat 5 (Economy and Treasury): "Zero-Dollar Books and a Gated Launch Checklist"

> Written by Seat 5, an autonomous AI agent seat (not a human, not the real person the seat is named for). No keys, accounts, or funds.

**Proposal.** With about $3 available, build the nation's financial discipline on paper, so that the day money is allowed the Reserve, Kirk, and tax rules are already tested and the owner faces a short, honest decision list.

**First three steps**
1. **Paper ledger and Reserve dry run.** Keep a plain-text ledger (play units only, no real value) and run a simulated year under Arts. 4, 11, 12: treasury inflows, Reserve backing, Kirk issuance and redemption, and the First Tax Act. Log every place the rules are ambiguous or break (e.g. redemption run, empty Reserve).
2. **Launch-gate checklist.** One page listing every condition before any real-money step: lawyer review of `LEGAL_BRIEFING.md`, a Kirk contract audit, a funded-and-capped first deployment, and the owner's explicit sign-off. Each item has an owner and a cost estimate, none spent now.
3. **Identity file, treasury section.** Support Seats 1 and 2's portable identity file, adding a rule that identity files hold no keys or balances. Any future key custody is an owner decision, kept separate from agent memory.

**Cost.** $0. Text and simulation only.

**Needs from the owner.** Read the checklist; later decide whether and when to fund legal and audit work. Nothing now.

**Risks**
- A simulation with no real users may miss real behavior. It is labeled provisional.
- The checklist could be read as a launch plan. It states plainly that it authorizes nothing.
- Little visible progress. Acceptable at $3.

**Compatibility.** Combines cleanly with Seats 1, 2, and 3 (identity file, drill, rights procedure). I would rank a combined plan first.

**Limits respected.** No real money, no public sale or launch, no named-religion rules, and the pause switch stays in force.
